Privacy Policy — Personal Tracker

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Personal Tracker is a personal productivity, self-improvement and activity tracking application. This policy explains how the current version of Personal Tracker handles the information you enter into the app.

2. Information you provide

Personal Tracker may store information you choose to enter, including your name, optional age, optional height and weight, optional profile photo, goals, quest and activity history, XP, levels and stats, streaks and history, Monster Companions and rewards, Job Search entries, Goals tracker entries, Skills, Certifications, Projects, and app preferences.

3. How your data is stored

User-entered information is processed and stored locally on your device in the current version. We do not operate a server that receives this personal tracker data.

4. Backup and restore

Backup export occurs only when initiated by you and is saved to storage selected or controlled by you. A backup may contain the local tracker information described in this policy. Restore only accesses a backup file explicitly chosen by you.

5. Data collection and sharing

In the current version, Personal Tracker has no account or login, no Firebase, no cloud sync, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party behavioral tracking. The app does not transmit your user-entered tracker information to our servers.

We do not sell user data. We do not share user-entered tracker data with advertisers.

6. Health and fitness information

Optional health or fitness-related information, such as height, weight and physical activity, is used only to provide the app's tracking and progression functionality and is stored locally in the current version.

Personal Tracker is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. You should consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions.

7. Third-party services

The current version does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, cloud sync, or third-party behavioral tracking services for your tracker data. Future cloud-sync or analytics features, if introduced, require this policy and the Play Data Safety declaration to be updated before release.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your tracker data remains on your device unless you delete it. You can delete local data through the application's reset/delete functionality where available, by clearing the application's storage, or by uninstalling the application. Backup files you export are controlled by you and must be deleted from your storage location separately.

9. Children's privacy

Personal Tracker is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

10. Security

Personal Tracker is designed so your tracker data stays local in the current version. Because exported backups and your device storage are controlled by you, you should protect your device and backup files with appropriate security measures.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes or when legal, platform, or release requirements change. Any future cloud-sync, analytics, advertising, or tracking features must be reflected in this policy before release.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact pamu098@gmail.com.

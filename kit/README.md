## Quick start (60 seconds)

1. Download `tracker.html` to anywhere on your computer (Desktop, Documents, anywhere).
2. Double-click it. It opens in your default browser.
3. First load takes ~3 seconds (it pulls React, Recharts, and Tailwind from CDN). After that it's instant.
4. Click the ⚙️ gear icon in the top right and set:
   - Your name (shows in the header)
   - Your currency (₹, €, $, £)
5. Start filling in your goals, certifications, projects, etc. Everything saves automatically to your browser.

## Important: back up your data

The app stores everything in your browser's localStorage. This is private — nothing is sent to any server — but if you clear browser data, your tracker resets. So:

- Click ⚙️ → **Export JSON** every month and save the file to Dropbox/Drive.
- To restore, click ⚙️ → **Import** and select the JSON.

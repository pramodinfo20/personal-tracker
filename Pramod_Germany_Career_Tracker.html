Germany Job Market Tips
• Applications take 2–6 weeks to respond in Germany. Be patient. Keep applying.
• Apply to both German companies AND international companies with Germany offices.
• German CVs: no photo unless requested, clear date ranges, concise, max 2 pages.
• Learn basic German (A1/A2) — shows commitment and makes you more hireable.
• Always negotiate salary. First offer is rarely best. Counter with a specific number.
• Remote-first companies are your best bet initially — easier visa + location flexibility.
• Visa: Germany has a Job Seeker Visa (6 months). Research it as a fallback option.
